## 🎯 What It Does

### Analyzes Your Data
- Field health scores
- Disease detections
- Weather conditions
- Growth stages

### Recommends Products
- Fertilizers for low health
- Fungicides for diseases
- Irrigation for dry soil
- Combo packs for multiple issues

### Prioritizes by Urgency
- 🚨 **URGENT**: Act now (disease, critical health)
- ⚠️ **RECOMMENDED**: Important (preventive, improvement)
- 💡 **SUGGESTED**: Helpful (optimization)

## 🛒 How to Use

### 1. View Recommendations
- Open dashboard
- Scroll to Smart Recommendations widget
- See personalized product suggestions

### 2. Buy a Product
- Click **"Buy Now"** button
- Navigate to marketplace
- Add to cart
- Checkout

### 3. Learn More
- Click **"Details"** button
- See full product information
- Read reviews & specs
- Decide to purchase

### 4. View All
- Click **"View All"** button
- See complete list of recommendations
- Filter and compare products

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: How often do recommendations update?
**A**: Every time you open the dashboard, based on latest field data.

### Q: Why am I seeing urgent recommendations?
**A**: Your field has a critical issue (disease, low health) that needs immediate attention.

### Q: What are combo packs?
**A**: Bundles of products that solve multiple problems together, with savings!

### Q: Can I ignore recommendations?
**A**: Yes, but urgent items should be addressed quickly to prevent crop damage.

### Q: How do I buy a product?
**A**: Click "Buy Now" → Add to cart → Checkout. Simple!
