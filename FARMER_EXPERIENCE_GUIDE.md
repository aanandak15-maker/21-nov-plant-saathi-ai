## 🎯 User Journey Examples

### **Journey 1: Morning Routine**

**7:00 AM - Farmer opens app**

1. **Sees:** "💧 Perfect time to water now!"
2. **Thinks:** "Good, I'll water my fields"
3. **Taps:** "My Fields" to see which fields need water
4. **Sees:** Field B has low moisture (32%)

**Time taken:** 30 seconds
**Result:** Farmer knows exactly what to do

**2:00 PM - Farmer notices yellow leaves**

1. **Taps:** "📸 Check Health" in bottom nav

**Time taken:** 2 minutes
**Result:** Problem identified and solution found

## 🎨 Visual Design Principles

### **1. Color Coding**
- 🔴 **Red** = Urgent (do now!)
- 🟠 **Orange** = Today (do today)
- 🔵 **Blue** = This week (plan ahead)
- 🟢 **Green** = Healthy (all good)
- 🟡 **Yellow** = Monitor (watch this)

### **2. Emoji Language**
- 💧 = Water
- 🌱 = Plant health
- 🦠 = Disease
- 📈 = Prices up
- 🌦️ = Weather
- 🧪 = Spray/fertilizer
- ⏰ = Time window

### **3. Simple Numbers**
- ❌ "NDVI: 0.75"
- ✅ "Health: 75%"

- ❌ "Soil moisture: 0.35"
- ✅ "Moisture: 35%"

### **4. Clear Actions**
- ❌ "Water stress detected"
- ✅ "Water your field today"

- ❌ "Fungal disease risk elevated"
- ✅ "Check leaves for spots"
