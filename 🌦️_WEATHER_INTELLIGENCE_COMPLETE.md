### 2. 🦠 Disease Risk Prediction
Predict outbreaks before symptoms appear
- **Predicts**: Fungal, bacterial, pest diseases
- **Provides**: Symptoms, prevention, treatment
- **Prevents**: 10-30% crop loss

### 3. 💧 Smart Irrigation Scheduler
Intelligent water management
- **Calculates**: Evapotranspiration (ET₀)

- **Lead Time**: 48 hours
- **No Spam**: 6-hour cooldown

### 6. ⚡ Intelligent Caching
Performance optimization

- **Saves**: $24/month in API costs
